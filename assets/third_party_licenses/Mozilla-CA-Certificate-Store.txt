Mozilla CA Certificate Store (cacert.pem)

Bestie bundles a copy of the Mozilla root CA certificate store so that its
HTTPS client (curl-impersonate) verifies TLS peers consistently across all
platforms. The bundle is the set of root certificates trusted by Mozilla,
extracted and published in PEM format by the curl project as "cacert.pem".

Certificate data (c) Mozilla Foundation and contributors.

Sources:
  - Bundle (cacert.pem): https://curl.se/ca/cacert.pem
  - Extraction docs:     https://curl.se/docs/caextract.html
  - Upstream data:       Mozilla NSS certdata.txt
  - Root store info:     https://wiki.mozilla.org/CA/Included_Certificates
